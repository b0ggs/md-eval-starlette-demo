Implemented the WebSocket disconnected-state contract.

- Added `WebSocketDisconnected`, a distinct `RuntimeError` subclass.
- Low-level receive/send now raise it after disconnection, preserving messages.
- Receive helpers raise it when the application side is not connected.
- Invalid modes/protocol errors remain `RuntimeError`; peer disconnects remain `WebSocketDisconnect`.
- Updated regression tests.

Validation: direct exception checks and compilation passed; one pytest smoke test passed. TestClient-based tests timed out in this environment.
